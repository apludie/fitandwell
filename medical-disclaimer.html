Medical Disclaimer

FIT and Well Living is an educational resource. Please read this important information about the limits of our content.

Last updated: October 9, 2026

In an emergency

If you think you may have a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room immediately.

Educational information only

The content on FIT and Well Living, including articles, guides, recipes, meal plans, calculators and any other material, is for general educational and informational purposes only. It is not intended to be, and should not be used as, a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Talk with a healthcare professional

Always seek the advice of your doctor, a registered dietitian or another qualified healthcare provider with any questions about a medical condition, your weight, your diet or physical activity. This is especially important if you:

  • Have a chronic health condition such as diabetes, heart disease, kidney disease or high blood pressure
  • Take prescription medications
  • Are pregnant, planning a pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Have a history of an eating disorder
  • Are under 18 or are an older adult
  • Are starting a new exercise program after being inactive

Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of something you read on FIT and Well Living.

Calculators

Our BMI, calorie, ideal weight and water intake calculators provide general estimates based on published formulas. They can't account for your full health history, body composition or individual needs, and they don't diagnose any condition.

No professional relationship

Using FIT and Well Living, or contacting us, does not create a doctor-patient, dietitian-client or any other professional relationship.

Individual results vary

Everyone's body and circumstances are different. We don't guarantee any particular result from following information on this website.

Eating disorder support

If you're struggling with your relationship with food or your body, you're not alone, and support is available. Please talk with your doctor or a mental health professional.

Accuracy

We work to keep our content accurate and up to date, but health research evolves. Read our editorial policy to learn how we create and update content.
