About FIT and Well Living

We help people in the United States build a healthier relationship with food, movement and weight through practical, trustworthy information.

Our mission

FIT and Well Living exists to make healthy weight management feel achievable. There's no shortage of conflicting advice, extreme diets and quick-fix promises online. We aim to offer the opposite: clear, balanced guidance and simple tools that help you make steady progress you can maintain.

What you'll find here

  • In-depth guides on weight loss, fat loss and belly fat.
  • Practical food ideas, including meal plans and healthy recipes with US measurements.
  • Free, private calculators for BMI, daily calories, ideal weight and water intake.
  • Articles on the habits that support a healthy weight, such as protein, sleep and handling plateaus, on our blog.

What we believe

  • Sustainable beats extreme. Small changes you can keep matter more than dramatic ones you can't.
  • Health is more than a number. Energy, strength, sleep and wellbeing count as progress too.
  • Everyone deserves respect. We never use shame or blame, and we write for people of all sizes and backgrounds.
  • Honesty first. We don't promise guaranteed results or recommend miracle products.

Our editorial approach

Our content is written to be accurate, practical and easy to understand. We base it on reputable sources, such as the Dietary Guidelines for Americans, the Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans and information from U.S. public health agencies. Read our full editorial policy to learn how we research, review and update our content.

Our team and reviewers

We believe you should know who is behind the information you read. As our team grows, we'll introduce our writers and any health professionals who review our content on this page, including their verified qualifications.

When an article has been reviewed by a credentialed professional, such as a registered dietitian or physician, the reviewer's name and credentials will appear on that article. Articles that don't list a reviewer have been prepared by the FIT and Well Living editorial team.

Not medical advice

FIT and Well Living provides general educational information. It's not a substitute for advice from your doctor or another qualified healthcare provider. Please read our medical disclaimer.
