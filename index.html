Our content standards

Careful, honest, evidence-informed

We want you to be able to trust what you read here, so we're transparent about how our content is made.

  • Grounded in public health guidance. We base our content on sources such as the Dietary Guidelines for Americans and U.S. public health agencies.
  • No miracle claims. We don't promote extreme diets, detoxes or guaranteed results.
  • Clear about review. When an article has been reviewed by a credentialed professional, we'll name the reviewer and their credentials on the page.
  • Kept up to date. We review articles regularly and correct errors promptly.
