What is BMI?

Body mass index (BMI) is a number calculated from your weight and height. In US units, the formula is BMI = 703 × weight (lb) ÷ height (in)². It's widely used as a quick screening tool because it's simple and inexpensive.

BMI categories for adults

Adult BMI categories
BMICategory
Below 18.5Underweight
18.5 to 24.9Healthy weight
25 to 29.9Overweight
30 or higherObesity

These categories apply to adults age 20 and older. Children and teens are assessed with age- and sex-specific BMI percentiles, so this calculator isn't designed for them.

Limitations of BMI

  • It doesn't measure body fat directly. Muscular people may have a high BMI without excess fat.
  • It doesn't show where fat is stored. Abdominal fat carries more health risk, which is why waist size adds useful information.
  • It can vary in meaning across groups. The relationship between BMI and health risk differs with age, sex and ethnicity. For example, some people of Asian descent may face higher health risks at lower BMIs.
  • It's one data point. Blood pressure, blood sugar, cholesterol, fitness and family history all matter too.
